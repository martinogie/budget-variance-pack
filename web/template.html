About the workbook

  • Ten tabs: Control, P&L Summary, Dept Opex, Revenue Bridge (with a waterfall chart), Headcount, Forecast, Commentary, and three data tabs (GL, payment-volume drivers, headcount).
  • 937 formulas, mostly SUMIFS over the GL extract. Change the reporting month on the Control tab and every report tab updates. No hard-coded results.
  • Inputs are in blue and the key assumption is in yellow. Links between tabs are in green. Variances are flagged with conditional formatting when they pass both thresholds.
  • Tests check that the recalculated workbook matches an independent pandas calculation, line by line.

All data is synthetic, generated from volume, take-rate and headcount drivers with known stories built in. Built with AI-assisted coding (Claude); the analysis, structure and review are my own.
