# September 2026 close: commentary

## Headline

- September EBITDA was $2.66M, +$0.21M vs budget. The beat is timing: the deferred Q3 marketing campaign (+$0.56M favourable this month) covers a $0.23M gross-profit shortfall.
- Year to date EBITDA is $24.93M, -$0.87M (-3.4%) vs budget. Revenue is ahead by $2.65M (+1.5%) but gross margin is 44.2% vs 45.6% budget.
- Full-year EBITDA outlook is $33.15M vs budget $35.31M (-$2.16M).

## Revenue

- Product revenue YTD +$1.82M: volume +$4.99M, mix -$1.10M, rate -$2.07M.
- Enterprise card volume is running ahead of plan after Q2 wins, but at a negotiated take rate of 2.19% vs 2.25% budget: rate effect -$2.00M YTD.
- SMB card volume is -3.3% vs plan YTD; shifting volume from SMB to lower-priced enterprise is the -$1.10M mix effect.
- Instant payouts +$0.74M and interest on client float +$0.98M (rates stayed higher than planned) are ahead.

## Cost of revenue

- Interchange and scheme fees -$2.72M YTD; volume driven, so they move with the revenue beat.
- Fraud and chargeback losses -$1.02M YTD, mostly the August card-testing attack ($850K). Velocity rules went live in early September; September was still -$159K vs budget.

## Operating expenses

- Engineering is 9 heads behind plan: salaries +$0.51M YTD (headcount effect +$0.82M, cost-per-head effect -$0.32M).
- Contractors -$0.67M YTD as engineering backfills open roles.
- Marketing programs +$1.06M YTD is timing: the Q3 campaign moved to Q4 and is in the outlook.
- Professional fees -$0.24M YTD for SOC 2 and money-transmitter licensing.
- 4 department lines breach the $50K and 5% thresholds this month (see Dept Opex).

## Actions

- Set a 2.20% floor on enterprise renewals and review pricing on the three largest new accounts.
- Close the engineering hiring gap by year end and roll off contractors as roles are filled.
- Confirm the Q4 campaign budget ($1.08M) with Marketing before 15 October.
- Keep the velocity fraud rules in place; review losses weekly through peak season.
